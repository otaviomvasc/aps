Root Cause: R0b + R6e — An Irreducible Infeasibility Certificate
The model fails at the LP relaxation stage, not due to integrality. The infeasibility is provably irreducible and is caused by the interaction of two constraints creating an impossible demand-capacity situation at three specific PHCs.

The Two Constraints That Clash
R0b — Closest-unit assignment rule:
ampls.t. R0b{i in I, j1 in L1: (i,j1) in Link01}:
    sum{k1 in L1: (i,k1) in Link01 and D0_1[i,k1] > D0_1[i,j1]} y0_1[i,k1]
    + y1[j1] <= 1;
```
Since `F1` forces `y1[j1] = 1` for all existing PHCs (`j1 ∈ EL[1]`), this collapses to:
```
sum{k1: farther than j1} y0_1[i,k1] = 0
Effect: every origin i can only be assigned to PHCs at most as far as its single closest existing EL[1] facility. This is not a soft preference — it is a hard zero on all farther assignments in the LP.
R6e / R9e — Capacity at existing PHC (with R1 flow balance):
ampls.t. R6e{j1 in EL[1]}: total_inflow[j1] <= C1[j1] + extra_size1[j1]*3000
Maximum capacity = SIZE=5 → 15,000 patients per PHC.

The Three Locked PHCs
After R0b is applied, 39 origins — totalling 77,213 patients — become locked to a single PHC with no alternative, and their forced demand exceeds that PHC's maximum expandable capacity:
PHCSIZE (current)C1 maxForced demandOverflow# locked clusters3118602220245215,00038,922+23,922193118602191059415,00021,693+6,693113118602190893415,00016,598+1,5989
For 3118602191059 and 3118602190893, none of the 22 candidate PHCs is closer to those locked clusters than the overloaded existing PHC — so even opening all candidates provides zero relief (R0b would still block routing to them, since they are farther away).
After accounting for the maximum possible 21% diversion to direct L2/L3 routes (MAX_HOME_SHC=0.15, MAX_HOME_THC=0.05), the irreducible minimum load at 3118602220245 is still 30,748 and at 3118602191059 is 17,137 — both far above the 15,000 ceiling. This is why the simplex terminates with a residual infeasibility of 4,698 in 128 rows: those rows are the flow-balance and capacity rows for the locked clusters that mathematically cannot be satisfied simultaneously.

Why the Data Validator Misses This
check_data_consistency.py checks only aggregate capacity (C2=4M ≥ demand ✓) and set membership. It is completely blind to R0b because the validator has no knowledge of the closest-unit assignment rule. The constraint is data-dependent and only activates when an existing PHC is the sole closest facility for a dense cluster of origins.

Fixes
Fix 1 — Remove or relax R0b (recommended, most impactful)
Comment out R0b entirely or change = 1 to <= 1 with a softer penalty in the objective. The constraint was intended as a "closest-unit preference" but as written it becomes a hard geometric lock.
Fix 2 — Raise the SIZE ceiling in aps.mod line 125 from <= 5 to <= 8 for the three overloaded PHCs. This raises C1_max from 15,000 to 24,000, enough to absorb 3118602191059 and 3118602190893. PHC 3118602220245 would need SIZE ≤ 13 (forced demand 38,922 after zero diversion), which may be unrealistic.
Fix 3 — Add candidate PHCs geographically closer to the locked clusters around 3118602220245 (clusters CLU_2926–CLU_3193). Currently candidates CLU_3200, CLU_3121, CLU_3176, CLU_3166 are reachable from those clusters but none is closer than 3118602220245, so R0b still blocks routing. A new candidate positioned within ~300–500m of the cluster centroid would intercept 17+ origins (W ≈ 27,377) and break the lock.
Fix 4 — Increase Dmax[1] from 5,000m to ≥ 7,000m. This widens Link01, giving locked origins additional existing EL[1] options within range. The R0b blocking distance would then be set by that farther existing PHC, which may have spare capacity.
